THE BRIDAL EDIT

"Designed for unforgettable beginnings."

The bridal trousseau is an intimate narrative of legacy, love, and ceremonial grace. Our Bridal Edit presents monumental drapes rendered in auspicious vermilion, deep burgundy, and royal emerald silks, woven with real electroplated gold zari that gleams with regal subtlety.

Pure Mulberry Silk
